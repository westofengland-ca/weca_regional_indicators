Indicators Summary

About These Summaries

These summaries present key regional indicators for the West of England Combined Authority’s priority areas. The indicators measure progress against the West of England Growth Strategy. They are not intended to directly measure outcomes or outputs for WECA projects, but rather to provide a high-level overview of regional trends and performance.
