Indicators Summary

About These Summaries

These summaries present key regional indicators for the West of England West of England’s priority areas. The indicators measure progress against the West of England Growth Strategy. They are not intended to directly measure outcomes or outputs for WECA projects, but rather to provide a high-level overview of regional trends and performance. A wide range of factors affect these indicators, including residents’ and businesses’ actions, long-term trends, national and international economic conditions, as well as regional interventions.

The default geography includes North Somerset as part of a wider West of England region unless otherwise stated. A process is underway to incorporate North Somerset into the West of England Combined Authority.

Priority Areas

  • Contributing to national economic growth helping our businesses succeed and creating jobs
  • Connecting the region through public transport & active travel
  • Building affordable and attractive homes in sustainable communities
  • Empowering residents with the skills to access the jobs that will shape our future
  • Making the West of England the home for green jobs and green growth
  • Lifting children and families out of poverty in the West of England
